Sit a full paper under exam conditions

Each paper has 50 questions covering all 7 weeks, 2 marks each, on a 3-hour timer. "Your paper" uses all {{mine_count}} of your own questions, topped up to 50 from the weeks they don't cover yet. Papers 1–3 come from the practice bank and share no questions. NPTEL doesn't publish this exam's exact pattern; confirm it on the instruction screen. The 40/100 line shown is the raw score that equals the 30/75 pass mark.

Practice mode marks each answer right or wrong as you go and has a Reveal answer button (a revealed question counts as wrong). Exam mode works like the real exam: no answers until you finish.
