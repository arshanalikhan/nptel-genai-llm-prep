A plan to the exam

The course runs 7 weeks of content: NLP basics, RNNs, attention and transformers, then generative models, LLMs, the generative AI project lifecycle (prompting, fine-tuning, PEFT, RLHF, RAG, LangChain, deployment) and responsible AI. Questions here are written fresh for this site from the official syllabus; NPTEL does not publish past proctored papers for this course.

Where the marks are. Weeks 3, 5 and 6 carry the most testable detail: scaled dot-product attention, decoding settings (temperature, top-k, top-p), prompting styles, LoRA and other PEFT methods, the RLHF pipeline, RAG and evaluation metrics. Learn the formulas on the Numericals page; they turn into easy 2-mark questions.
